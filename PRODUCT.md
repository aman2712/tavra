# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employees handling an urgent work-travel disruption from their phone. They are
often time-constrained, away from their luggage, and trying to stay ready for a
client commitment. They interact with Tavra primarily through iMessage and
should only leave the conversation for a security-sensitive action that cannot
be completed safely in chat.

## Product Purpose

Tavra turns company knowledge, employee preferences, travel context, and policy
limits into a guided recovery workflow. It identifies what the employee needs,
confirms stored details, finds an eligible option, and prepares a secure payment
authorization with minimal typing or navigation. Success means the employee can
recover from a disruption through a short, trustworthy conversation and approve
payment without exposing card details to Tavra.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and reassuring. Tavra should feel as polished and dependable as
Apple Wallet while retaining the warmth of a thoughtful human helping over text.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dense enterprise dashboard, a generic chatbot transcript, a
crypto wallet, or a conventional multi-step checkout full of forms and buttons.
Avoid cluttered summaries, decorative fintech effects, aggressive urgency,
technical payment terminology, and interfaces that make the employee re-enter
known information.

## Design Principles

1. Ask for confirmation before asking for input.
2. Keep the employee in iMessage until secure interaction is necessary.
3. Make the next action obvious without making the interface feel automated.
4. Reveal complexity progressively and keep payment mechanics out of the way.
5. State clearly what has and has not been authorized, purchased, or completed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic status
announcements, large touch targets, keyboard operation, and reduced-motion
preferences. Never communicate status through color alone.
